# Support Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

This page is the **Support Policy** at `/legal/support`. It explains the scope of support PROOVRA offers, how requests are routed, how severity is prioritized, and the boundaries support cannot cross.

PROOVRA maintains two distinct support surfaces:

- **Public Support page** — `/support` — the operational contact and customer help surface where users can find help, FAQ, and contact details
- **Support Policy** — `/legal/support` — this document, describing scope, expectations, and boundaries

Both surfaces remain accessible. This Support Policy is the legal-document version. The Public Support page is the operational entry point.

## 1. CONTACT CHANNELS

The right contact depends on the type of request. The matrix below is the canonical routing table.

| Request type | Contact / route | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| General product support | [support@proovra.com](mailto:support@proovra.com) or [/support](/support) | Account access, upload issues, report questions, configuration help |
| Billing support | [billing@proovra.com](mailto:billing@proovra.com) or [/support](/support) | Subscriptions, invoices, payment processor questions, refund requests |
| Legal and privacy | [legal@proovra.com](mailto:legal@proovra.com) / [privacy@proovra.com](mailto:privacy@proovra.com) | Data-subject requests, legal notices, policy questions |
| Security reports | [security@proovra.com](mailto:security@proovra.com) | Vulnerabilities, suspected compromise, responsible disclosure |
| Abuse reports | [support@proovra.com](mailto:support@proovra.com) plus the Abuse Reporting policy | Misuse, unlawful content, fraudulent verification use |
| Law-enforcement requests | [legal@proovra.com](mailto:legal@proovra.com) plus the Law Enforcement Request Policy | Legal process, preservation requests, formal demands |
| Sales / enterprise | [/contact-sales](/contact-sales) | Enterprise evaluation, procurement, security review |

Use the channel that best matches the request type. Requests sent to the wrong channel may be routed internally.

## 2. SUPPORT SCOPE MATRIX

The matrix below summarizes what support may help with and what support does not do. The scope reflects PROOVRA's role as a digital evidence operations platform, not as a court, regulator, forensic-investigation authority, or legal-advice provider.

| Area | Support may help with | Support does not do |
|---|---|---|
| Account access | Login guidance, MFA recovery guidance, workspace access questions | Bypass customer-controlled access controls without authorization |
| Billing | Plan and subscription questions, invoice review, payment-provider routing, refund eligibility under the Refund Policy | Override statutory or contractual billing rules |
| Evidence workflows | Upload, capture, report, and verification guidance | Determine factual truth, lawful provenance, or legal admissibility of uploaded material |
| Verification outputs | Explain Verification Reports, Verification Packages, Public Verification URLs, and status labels | Certify authenticity, identity, authorship, or court acceptance |
| Teams and workspaces | Role and permission guidance, SSO and SCIM configuration help where supported | Decide who should have access inside the customer's organization |
| Security | Receive and route suspected compromise reports, guide credential rotation | Provide emergency monitoring, real-time security operations, or guaranteed recovery |
| Abuse and legal | Route reports to the correct policy channel | Act as a court, law-enforcement body, or legal advisor |

## 3. SEVERITY AND PRIORITIZATION

Support requests are prioritized by severity, risk, plan, contractual commitments, and operational capacity. The levels below describe how PROOVRA aims to triage. They do not, by themselves, create a guaranteed response or resolution time.

| Priority | Examples | Handling expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Suspected account compromise, security incident, severe service-impacting issue | Prioritized review and escalation |
| High | Billing lockout, workspace access issue, report-generation failure affecting an active review | Prioritized support queue |
| Normal | Product questions, configuration help, non-urgent verification questions | Handled in the ordinary support queue |
| Low | Feedback, documentation suggestions, non-urgent usability questions | Reviewed as capacity allows |

PROOVRA aims to prioritize requests based on severity, risk, plan, contractual commitments, and operational capacity. Unless an applicable enterprise agreement states otherwise, this policy does not create a guaranteed response or resolution time.

## 4. ENTERPRISE SUPPORT

Enterprise customers may have additional support terms in an order form, Master Services Agreement, Data Processing Addendum, or enterprise agreement.

- Contracted support commitments override this public policy only for that customer.
- Enterprise support may include security-review assistance, procurement documentation, DPA and subprocessor questions, onboarding guidance, and workspace configuration guidance.
- PROOVRA does not promise 24/7 support, dedicated named contacts, or named-engineer response unless contracted.

For enterprise evaluation and procurement, contact [/contact-sales](/contact-sales).

## 5. ABUSE ESCALATION

Abuse reports are routed according to the [Abuse Reporting](/legal/abuse-reporting) policy.

Where appropriate, PROOVRA may:

- restrict access to affected content
- preserve records relating to the matter
- suspend accounts or workspaces
- coordinate with subprocessors and competent authorities

PROOVRA does not adjudicate user disputes, determine factual truth, or act as a legal authority. The Abuse Reporting policy governs how reports are intake, reviewed, and resolved.

## 6. SECURITY ESCALATION

Suspected vulnerabilities should be reported to **security@proovra.com** under the [Security and Responsible Disclosure](/legal/security) policy.

Where account compromise is suspected, PROOVRA may:

- restrict the affected session or sessions
- guide credential reset and MFA re-enrolment
- review relevant audit logs and custody events
- escalate to the incident-response process where indicators warrant it

Security incidents involving personal data or service security are handled under the [Incident Response Policy](/legal/incident-response).

## 7. LEGAL, PRIVACY, AND LAW-ENFORCEMENT ROUTING

- **Privacy and data-subject requests** — [privacy@proovra.com](mailto:privacy@proovra.com) and the [Privacy Requests](/legal/privacy-requests) page.
- **Legal demands and law-enforcement process** — [legal@proovra.com](mailto:legal@proovra.com) and the [Law Enforcement Request Policy](/legal/law-enforcement).
- **Copyright notices and counter-notices** — the [Copyright and DMCA Policy](/legal/dmca).
- **Transparency-related questions** — the [Transparency Policy](/legal/transparency).

Support does not provide legal advice, interpret legal admissibility, or substitute for counsel.

## 8. BILLING ESCALATION

Billing issues may involve Stripe or PayPal where applicable, as listed on the [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.

- Refund and cancellation requests follow the [Consumer Cancellation and Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy) or the applicable enterprise contract.
- PROOVRA does not store raw payment-card data in native form; payment data is handled through the relevant payment processor's checkout surface.
- Subscription, invoicing, and tax questions are routed through [billing@proovra.com](mailto:billing@proovra.com).

## 9. AVAILABILITY DISCLAIMER

Support availability depends on operational capacity, queue volume, the service plan in question, holidays, ongoing incidents, and the availability of third-party providers listed in the [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.

- This policy does not create a guaranteed resolution time unless expressly contracted.
- AI feature unavailability does not block core verification, custody, or reporting workflows; the integrity layer functions independently of AI.
- Where outages affect support availability, PROOVRA seeks to provide reasonable status communication.

## 10. WHAT SUPPORT CANNOT DO

Support does not:

- provide legal advice
- provide forensic testimony
- provide emergency response services
- provide real-time personal-safety monitoring
- issue court-acceptance or admissibility opinions
- determine factual truth, authorship, identity, intent, or lawful provenance
- guarantee recovery of deleted content
- bypass access controls, MFA, or workspace boundaries
- act as law enforcement, a regulator, or a forensic-investigation authority

Where a request falls outside the support scope, support may explain the boundary and suggest the correct channel.

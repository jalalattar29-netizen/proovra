# Transparency Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

PROOVRA seeks to provide reasonable transparency around legal demands, abuse handling, copyright complaints, government requests, security disclosures, and governance practices relevant to a trust-sensitive digital evidence operations platform.

This policy describes the principles PROOVRA applies. It is read together with the Privacy Policy, the Law Enforcement Request Policy, the Abuse Reporting page, the Privacy Requests page, the Security and Responsible Disclosure policy, the Incident Response Policy, and the Trust Center.

## 1. SCOPE

This policy covers PROOVRA's handling of:

- law-enforcement, court, regulator, and government requests
- abuse and unlawful-content reports
- copyright and DMCA-style notices
- security reports (responsible disclosure)
- privacy and data-subject requests
- preservation requests issued by competent authority

This policy describes principles. The detailed process for each request type lives in the relevant individual policy.

## 2. PRINCIPLES

PROOVRA's approach to these requests follows the following principles.

### 2.1 Legal Review

Requests are reviewed for valid legal process, scope, jurisdiction, and proportionality before any disclosure or action.

### 2.2 Minimum-Necessary Disclosure

Where disclosure is required, PROOVRA seeks to disclose the minimum information necessary to comply with the request.

### 2.3 Jurisdiction Review

PROOVRA reviews requests against the legal regime of the jurisdiction in which PROOVRA is established, the jurisdiction issuing the request, the jurisdiction of the data subject where reasonably ascertainable, and any applicable customer agreement or DPA.

### 2.4 User Notice

Where legally permitted, operationally feasible, and consistent with user-safety considerations, PROOVRA may notify affected users before disclosure. Notice may be delayed or omitted where required by law, where notice would compromise an active investigation, or where notice would create a risk of harm to a person.

### 2.5 Preservation Without Disclosure

Where competent authorities issue a preservation request consistent with applicable law, PROOVRA may preserve specified records pending receipt of valid legal process. Preservation does not by itself authorise disclosure.

### 2.6 No Voluntary Bulk Disclosure

PROOVRA does not voluntarily provide bulk access to user data, content, or evidence material to authorities. Disclosure is made only in response to specific, lawful requests.

### 2.7 Challenging Overbroad Requests

PROOVRA may seek to narrow, push back on, or challenge requests that PROOVRA reasonably believes are overbroad, defective, lacking jurisdiction, or otherwise inconsistent with applicable law, through appropriate channels.

### 2.8 Customer Notice for Processor Workspaces

Where a request relates to data inside a customer workspace and PROOVRA acts as a processor, PROOVRA will, where legally permitted, route the request to the customer or notify the customer so the customer may respond as controller.

### 2.9 Evidence-Content Boundary

PROOVRA does not interpret the factual content of user-uploaded materials, does not endorse or certify their factual truth, authorship, identity, intent, liability, or admissibility, and does not act as a court, regulator, or forensic-investigation authority.

## 3. POSSIBLE AGGREGATE REPORTING

PROOVRA may, from time to time, publish a transparency report describing the platform's overall handling of authority requests. PROOVRA does not commit to a specific schedule, format, or scope for any such publication. Where published, a transparency report may include aggregated, anonymised information such as:

- the categories of requests received
- approximate request counts in aggregated form
- approximate compliance, rejection, and partial-compliance counts
- approximate counts of emergency requests
- approximate counts of preservation requests
- general commentary on jurisdictional or process trends

Any aggregate reporting is anonymised. PROOVRA does not publish information that would identify specific authorities, individuals, customers, accounts, or evidence records, except where required by applicable law.

The phrase "may publish" is intentional. This section describes what PROOVRA may do, not what PROOVRA commits to do.

## 4. NATIONAL SECURITY AND GAG ORDERS

Where PROOVRA receives a request subject to a non-disclosure obligation under applicable law, PROOVRA seeks to comply with the non-disclosure obligation while applying the principles of this policy. Where lawful, aggregate information about such requests may be included in transparency reporting consistent with applicable law.

## 5. LIMITATIONS

PROOVRA may delay, limit, or omit transparency reporting where prohibited by law, where disclosure would create security risks, where operational constraints apply, or where reporting could compromise an active investigation.

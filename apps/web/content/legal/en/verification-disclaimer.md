# Verification Disclaimer

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

This short notice describes the boundaries of what PROOVRA verification surfaces and reports actually assert. It is intended to be linked from reports, verification pages, and the [Verification Methodology](/legal/verification-methodology) document.

## 1. WHAT PROOVRA VERIFIES

PROOVRA surfaces **recorded technical and custody signals** associated with an evidence record. Where configured and where available, those signals may include:

- the SHA-256 fingerprint computed at intake and recomputed at verification
- timestamp-related context, including RFC 3161 trusted timestamps and OpenTimestamps anchoring state where available
- digital signature materials for the record fingerprint where available
- custody event history captured in a hash-chained log
- preservation-related indicators, such as immutable storage state where configured
- reports and verification packages generated from the recorded materials

These signals help reviewers inspect what the platform recorded for a given evidence record.

## 2. WHAT PROOVRA DOES NOT DETERMINE

PROOVRA does not by itself determine:

- factual truth
- authorship
- identity
- intent
- liability
- legal admissibility
- court acceptance
- authenticity of real-world events

Those determinations remain subject to the responsible court, investigator, regulator, insurer, employer, or expert process, applying the relevant legal, evidentiary, and operational standards of the jurisdiction or matter.

## 3. IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION

PROOVRA is not a court, law-enforcement authority, or legal service provider. Verification confirms recorded integrity signals, timestamp-related context, custody metadata, and preservation-related details for an evidence record. It does not by itself establish factual truth, authorship, identity, liability, or legal admissibility in a specific jurisdiction.

## 4. REVIEWER RESPONSIBILITY

Reviewers, claimants, counsel, regulators, and other recipients of PROOVRA-generated materials are expected to:

- evaluate the underlying recorded content on its own merits
- apply the relevant standards of the matter, jurisdiction, or process
- treat PROOVRA-generated signals as supporting context rather than conclusive determinations

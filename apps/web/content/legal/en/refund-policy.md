# Consumer Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

This policy describes how cancellation and refunds work for individual consumers who purchase a paid PROOVRA plan. Where you contracted with PROOVRA under an enterprise agreement, the cancellation, termination, and refund terms of that agreement govern instead of this policy.

This policy is intended as plain-language guidance. Statutory rights under European Union and German consumer law continue to apply where applicable; this page does not waive those rights.

## 1. SCOPE

This policy applies to:

- consumers who purchase a paid individual plan directly from PROOVRA
- consumers who purchase an add-on, credit pack, or upgrade directly from PROOVRA

This policy does not apply to:

- enterprise contracts or order forms (governed by the relevant signed agreement)
- third-party app store purchases (governed by the relevant app store policy)
- trial or free-tier usage (no charge, no refund needed)

## 2. RIGHT OF WITHDRAWAL FOR DIGITAL SERVICES

Under EU consumer law, consumers ordinarily have a 14-day right of withdrawal for distance contracts.

For digital services such as PROOVRA, this right of withdrawal **expires once the consumer has expressly requested that the service start during the withdrawal period and has acknowledged that the right of withdrawal is lost upon full performance**. PROOVRA may present this acknowledgement during checkout for instantly-provisioned digital services.

Where the right of withdrawal has not been waived in this way, the consumer may withdraw from the contract within 14 days of order confirmation by contacting **billing@proovra.com**.

## 3. CANCELLATION

You may cancel a recurring paid plan at any time from the billing area of your account, or by contacting **billing@proovra.com**.

Cancellation stops future renewal charges. Cancellation does not, by itself, generate a refund for the current billing period.

## 4. REFUNDS

Where a refund is requested:

- requests made within the withdrawal window (where the right of withdrawal has not been waived) are processed in line with the applicable consumer law
- requests outside the withdrawal window are reviewed individually; refunds may be granted at PROOVRA's discretion for unused billing periods, duplicate charges, or service issues recognized by PROOVRA's support team
- credit packs and similar prepaid amounts may be non-refundable once partially consumed, except where required by applicable law

Refunds, where granted, are normally returned to the original payment method.

## 5. DELETION AND LEGAL/AUDIT RECORDS

Cancellation or refund does not automatically delete:

- audit logs, custody events, or operational logs that PROOVRA is required to retain under applicable law or under the Privacy Policy and Data Retention Policy
- records subject to a legal hold or to retention obligations of a customer workspace
- materials that PROOVRA is required to preserve for security, fraud prevention, or legal investigations

See the [Data Retention Policy](/legal/data-retention) for retention boundaries.

## 6. ENTERPRISE CONTRACTS

Enterprise customers should consult the cancellation, termination, and refund provisions of the relevant order form, master subscription agreement, or other signed agreement. This consumer policy does not modify those provisions.

## 7. CONTACT

Cancellation and refund requests: **billing@proovra.com**.

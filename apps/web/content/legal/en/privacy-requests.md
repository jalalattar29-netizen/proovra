# Privacy Requests

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

This page explains how individuals can exercise their privacy rights with respect to personal data processed by PROOVRA, and how PROOVRA handles those requests under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG) where applicable.

For the full description of how personal data is processed, see the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). For how PROOVRA acts as a processor for customer-controlled evidence content, see the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa).

## 1. WHO THIS APPLIES TO

This page applies to data subjects whose personal data is processed by PROOVRA in connection with the operation of the website, account creation, account management, customer support, and related service-level processing.

Where evidence content uploaded by a customer contains personal data of a third party, the customer is normally the controller for that content. Requests about evidence content should usually be directed to the customer (the controller). PROOVRA will assist the customer where required by the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa).

## 2. RIGHTS YOU MAY EXERCISE

Subject to applicable law and to any limits set out in the Privacy Policy, you may request:

- **access** to your personal data
- **rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- **deletion** of your personal data
- **restriction** of processing
- **portability** of your personal data, where applicable
- **objection** to processing
- **withdrawal of consent** where processing is based on consent (without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out)

Some rights may be limited by other legal obligations, including, for example, retention of audit-related material, legal hold, prevention of abuse, security investigations, or other obligations recognized under applicable law.

## 3. HOW TO SUBMIT A REQUEST

Send your request to **privacy@proovra.com** with:

- a clear description of the right you are exercising
- enough information to identify your personal data (such as the email address used with PROOVRA)
- where applicable, the workspace, account, or context the request relates to

To protect the rights of data subjects, PROOVRA may need to verify the identity of the requester. Verification may include confirming control of an associated email address or providing additional information sufficient to establish a reasonable link to the data subject.

## 4. RESPONSE TIME

PROOVRA aims to respond to a valid privacy request within **one month** of receiving the request and any required verification information. Where a request is complex or PROOVRA receives a large number of requests, this period may be extended by up to two further months where the extension is legally permitted. The requester will be informed of any such extension and the reasons for it.

## 5. SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY

If you believe your data protection rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority in the EU/EEA Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

For data subjects in Germany, the competent supervisory authority depends on the federal state. Where the relevant authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia, the authority is:

**Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW)**

Please consult the authority's published guidance for current contact details and submission instructions.

## 6. REQUESTS RELATING TO CUSTOMER WORKSPACES

Where the request concerns personal data inside a customer workspace (for example, content uploaded as evidence by a customer), PROOVRA will normally direct the requester to the customer who controls that workspace, and will assist the customer as required by the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa).

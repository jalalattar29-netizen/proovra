# Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

PROOVRA is committed to making its public website and product surfaces usable for as wide an audience as reasonably possible, including users with disabilities. This statement describes PROOVRA's accessibility approach, its objectives, its known limitations, and how to provide feedback.

This statement does not constitute a certification of conformance with any specific accessibility standard. Statements about accessibility objectives use language such as "objective", "goal", and "target" because PROOVRA does not claim certified or audited conformance unless that conformance has been independently verified for a named release.

## 1. SCOPE

This statement applies to:

- the public marketing website
- the public verification interfaces (where supported)
- the operator-facing application, to the extent reasonably supported
- the published reviewer-facing surfaces such as Verification Reports and Public Verification URLs, to the extent reasonably supported

This statement does not cover third-party content embedded in user-uploaded evidence material, which is controlled by customers and is outside PROOVRA's design control. It also does not cover features delivered exclusively through third-party providers (for example, federated sign-in surfaces operated by third-party identity providers).

## 2. ACCESSIBILITY OBJECTIVES

PROOVRA's objective is to design product surfaces that:

- can be used with a keyboard alone for primary tasks
- maintain readable colour contrast on key reading surfaces
- structure pages with meaningful headings and landmarks
- expose accessible names for primary interactive controls
- accept text scaling without breaking layout where reasonably possible
- avoid effects (rapid flashing, motion, autoplay) that are known to cause harm to users with photosensitive conditions
- allow assistive technologies to operate against primary product surfaces

PROOVRA's stated objective is to align primary product surfaces with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA** success criteria over time, treated as a design target rather than as a certified state. PROOVRA may publish progress updates from time to time. The use of the word "objective" reflects an intention; it should not be read as a binding certification.

## 3. ACCESSIBILITY ROADMAP

The roadmap below describes the areas PROOVRA seeks to address. Roadmap items are aspirational and may shift based on priority, technical feasibility, and feedback.

- **Foundations.** Establish consistent semantic structure, landmark roles, accessible names, and focus management on primary marketing and product surfaces.
- **Keyboard navigation.** Confirm that primary product tasks can be completed with the keyboard alone, with clear focus indicators.
- **Screen-reader compatibility.** Improve programmatic exposure of interactive components and content order for primary surfaces.
- **Colour and contrast.** Review reading surfaces for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast targets and adjust where not yet aligned.
- **Reduced motion.** Respect user "reduced motion" preferences for non-essential animations.
- **Form design.** Improve field labels, error messages, and error recovery for sign-in, billing, and request flows.
- **Evidence visualisation.** Iterate on accessibility of evidence visualisation, signed Verification Packages, and reviewer dashboards, which may have higher technical complexity than reading surfaces.
- **Documentation.** Improve accessibility of published policy documents and Trust Center pages.

## 4. KNOWN LIMITATIONS

PROOVRA acknowledges that some surfaces may not yet meet its accessibility objectives, including:

- complex evidence visualisation and reviewer dashboards
- some signed Verification Package or download interactions
- some interactive diagrams or technical illustrations
- some third-party-hosted components

PROOVRA continues to iterate on these surfaces. If you encounter an accessibility issue, please tell us — see Section 6.

## 5. ASSISTIVE TECHNOLOGY TESTING

PROOVRA may exercise primary product surfaces with assistive technologies (such as common screen readers, keyboard-only interaction, and high-contrast modes) as part of its development process. Coverage is not exhaustive and may vary by release. Where a recent assistive-technology check has been performed for a release, PROOVRA may publish a brief note describing the scope of that check.

## 6. FEEDBACK PROCESS

We welcome accessibility feedback. Please contact:

**support@proovra.com**

Include, where possible:

- the page or surface you were using (URL or feature name)
- the assistive technology, browser, and operating system you were using
- a description of the issue and the outcome you expected
- any relevant screenshots or recordings

PROOVRA aims to acknowledge accessibility feedback in a reasonable time and to route it to the team responsible for the affected surface. Accessibility feedback is reviewed alongside other product issues and is used to inform ongoing improvements.

## 7. ALTERNATIVE FORMATS

If you need a PROOVRA-published document in an alternative format that is more accessible to you (for example, a plain-text rendering of a Verification Report), please contact **accessibility@proovra.com**. PROOVRA aims to provide a reasonable alternative where feasible.

## 8. NO CERTIFICATION CLAIM

PROOVRA does not claim:

- certified accessibility conformance
- audited WCAG compliance
- accredited assistive-technology compatibility

unless that claim has been independently verified for a named release. Statements in this document describe intentions and design targets.

## 9. THIRD-PARTY CONTENT

Where third-party content is presented inside PROOVRA — for example, federated sign-in surfaces operated by Google or Apple, payment surfaces operated by Stripe, or evidence content uploaded by users — the accessibility of that content depends on the third-party provider or the customer, not on PROOVRA's design. PROOVRA seeks to surface third-party components alongside accessible PROOVRA-controlled surfaces wherever reasonably possible.

## 10. REGULATORY CONTEXT

Accessibility expectations may differ by jurisdiction. PROOVRA seeks to act consistently with applicable accessibility law and reasonable industry expectations. Specific regulatory determinations remain a matter for competent authorities.

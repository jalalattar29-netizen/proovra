# Law Enforcement Request Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

This policy describes how PROOVRA responds to requests from law-enforcement agencies, regulators, courts, and other governmental authorities ("authorities").

It is intended to clarify the platform's approach to legal process, user rights, disclosure restraint, and transparency. This policy does not waive any rights of PROOVRA, its customers, or its users under applicable law.

## 1. GENERAL PRINCIPLES

PROOVRA respects user rights, applicable law, and due process. PROOVRA discloses user data only where it determines that disclosure is legally required or otherwise permitted under applicable law, taking into account proportionality, minimum-necessary disclosure, jurisdictional review, and customer trust.

Where the request relates to data inside a customer workspace, PROOVRA may direct the requesting authority to the customer who controls that workspace, unless legal process requires otherwise.

## 2. VALID LEGAL PROCESS

PROOVRA may respond to requests including:

- court orders
- subpoenas (or local equivalents)
- legally binding governmental requests
- search warrants where issued by competent authority
- preservation requests issued by competent authority
- emergency requests where legally permissible (see Section 7)
- other lawful demands from competent authorities

Requests must be appropriately scoped, signed by a competent official, and addressed to PROOVRA at **legal@proovra.com**. PROOVRA may decline or push back on requests that are facially defective, overbroad, lacking jurisdiction, or otherwise inconsistent with applicable law.

## 3. JURISDICTION REVIEW

PROOVRA reviews requests against the legal regime of:

- the jurisdiction in which PROOVRA is established
- the jurisdiction issuing the request
- the jurisdiction of the data subject, where reasonably ascertainable
- any applicable customer agreement or DPA

Where conflicts arise, PROOVRA may seek to resolve them under applicable law, including by requesting clarification, by routing through mutual legal assistance mechanisms, or by challenging the request where appropriate.

## 4. INFORMATION THAT MAY BE AVAILABLE

Depending on the account, workflow, and retention state, PROOVRA may hold information such as:

- account registration data (name, email, account identifiers)
- workspace and team membership
- service usage metadata
- logs and access records (subject to retention periods in the Data Retention Policy)
- evidence-related technical verification data (hashes, signatures, custody events, timestamp metadata, Verification Reports, Verification Package manifests)
- billing-related information
- support communications relevant to the matter

PROOVRA does not guarantee that any particular category of data exists, is retained, or is available in a given case. Data not retained at the time of the request cannot be produced.

## 5. EVIDENCE CONTENT BOUNDARY

PROOVRA provides technical integrity and verification tooling. PROOVRA does not:

- independently interpret the factual content of user-uploaded materials
- endorse or certify the factual truth, authorship, identity, intent, or admissibility of any uploaded content
- act as a court, regulator, or forensic-investigation authority

The Verification Methodology and Verification Disclaimer set out the boundaries of what PROOVRA records and verifies.

## 6. PRESERVATION REQUESTS

Where competent authorities issue a preservation request consistent with applicable law, PROOVRA may preserve specified records for a defined period pending receipt of valid legal process. Preservation does not constitute disclosure. A preservation request does not by itself authorise PROOVRA to release the preserved records to the requesting authority.

## 7. EMERGENCY REQUESTS

In limited cases involving imminent risk of death, serious physical harm, or similar emergency situations, PROOVRA may assess emergency disclosure requests as permitted by applicable law. Emergency requests should:

- be submitted to **legal@proovra.com** with "EMERGENCY" in the subject line
- include the requesting agency name and an official contact channel for verification
- include a clear, contemporaneous description of the imminent risk
- identify the records sought as narrowly as possible

PROOVRA may decline or limit emergency disclosure where the request fails to demonstrate imminent risk, where verification of the requesting agency is not possible, or where applicable law does not permit the disclosure.

## 8. MINIMUM-NECESSARY DISCLOSURE

PROOVRA seeks to disclose only the minimum information necessary to comply with the legal request. Where a request is overbroad, PROOVRA may seek narrowing, push back, or challenge the request through appropriate channels.

## 9. CHALLENGING OVERBROAD REQUESTS

Where PROOVRA reasonably believes a request is overbroad, defective, lacking jurisdiction, or otherwise inconsistent with applicable law, PROOVRA may seek to:

- narrow the request through dialogue with the issuing authority
- request clarification or additional process
- challenge the request through judicial or administrative review where appropriate
- refer the matter to legal counsel

## 10. USER NOTICE

Where legally permitted, operationally feasible, and consistent with user safety considerations, PROOVRA may notify the affected user before disclosing data in response to a request. Notice may be delayed or omitted where:

- the request prohibits notice
- notice is restricted by applicable law
- notice would compromise an active investigation
- notice would create a risk of harm to a person
- notice would frustrate the purpose of the request

## 11. NO VOLUNTARY BULK DISCLOSURE

PROOVRA does not voluntarily provide bulk access to user data, content, or evidence material to authorities. Disclosure is made only in response to specific, lawful requests, subject to the principles in this policy.

## 12. CUSTOMER COOPERATION

Where the request relates to data inside a customer workspace and PROOVRA acts as a processor for that customer, PROOVRA will, where legally permitted, route the request to the customer or notify the customer so that the customer may respond as controller. Exceptions apply where legal process requires PROOVRA to respond directly without notice.

## 13. TRANSPARENCY REPORTING

### 13.1 Possible Transparency Reporting

PROOVRA may publish, from time to time, a transparency report describing the platform's overall handling of authority requests. PROOVRA does not commit to any specific publication schedule, format, or scope. Where a transparency report is published, it may include:

- the categories of requests received
- approximate request counts in aggregated form
- approximate compliance, rejection, and partial-compliance counts
- approximate counts of emergency requests
- approximate counts of preservation requests
- general commentary on jurisdictional or process trends

### 13.2 Aggregated and Anonymised Only

Any transparency reporting is aggregated and anonymised. PROOVRA does not publish information that would identify specific authorities, individuals, customers, accounts, or evidence records, except where required by applicable law.

### 13.3 Non-Commitment Language

This policy describes what PROOVRA **may** publish. PROOVRA does not commit to publishing transparency information on any particular schedule, in any particular format, or at all. Statements in this section should not be interpreted as a binding commitment.

## 14. NATIONAL SECURITY AND GAG ORDERS

Where PROOVRA receives a request subject to a non-disclosure obligation under applicable law, PROOVRA seeks to comply with the non-disclosure obligation while applying the principles of this policy. Where lawful, aggregate information about such requests may be included in transparency reporting consistent with applicable law.

## 15. CONTACT

Law-enforcement and governmental authorities should send requests to:

**legal@proovra.com**

with appropriate references to the issuing authority, the legal basis, the records sought, and a verifiable channel for follow-up.

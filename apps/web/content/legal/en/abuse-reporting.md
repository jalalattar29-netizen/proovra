# Abuse and Unlawful Content Reporting

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

This policy explains how to report misuse of PROOVRA, unlawful content on the platform, or abuse of evidence workflows and verification features, and how PROOVRA processes those reports.

PROOVRA is a digital evidence operations platform. It is intended to support lawful evidence capture, preservation, verification, and review. Misuse of the platform, including unlawful use of evidence features, is prohibited under the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 1. SCOPE

PROOVRA may review reports relating to:

- illegal or unlawful content
- privacy violations
- harassment or unlawful surveillance using evidence features
- impersonation or identity-misrepresentation
- fraudulent or misleading use of evidence records, Verification Reports, Verification Packages, or Public Verification URLs
- abuse of the verification system to deceive recipients
- distribution of malware, account abuse, credential abuse, or platform attacks
- intellectual-property violations (see also the Copyright and DMCA Policy for copyright-specific notices)
- material that depicts the exploitation of children (which PROOVRA additionally addresses under applicable law and with competent authorities)

## 2. HOW TO REPORT

Please send reports to **support@proovra.com** with a clear subject line referencing the type of report (for example: "Abuse report — fraudulent verification record").

Where relevant, include:

- the relevant URL, Public Verification URL, Verification Package identifier, evidence record identifier, or workspace identifier
- a description of the issue
- why you believe the content or activity is unlawful, abusive, or in breach of platform terms
- supporting screenshots or reference materials where appropriate
- your contact details if you would like a follow-up
- where applicable, your relationship to the matter (for example, affected individual, authorised representative, journalist, researcher, public authority)

For copyright-specific notices, please use the contact details in the Copyright and DMCA Policy.

For privacy and data-subject requests, please use the Privacy Requests page.

For law-enforcement and government requests, please use the Law Enforcement Request Policy.

For security vulnerabilities, please use the Security and Responsible Disclosure policy.

## 3. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

PROOVRA aims to acknowledge receipt of abuse reports within a reasonable time after submission. Acknowledgement does not constitute a determination on the merits of the report.

## 4. REVIEW PROCESS

### 4.1 Initial Triage

Reports are triaged on the basis of available information, severity, evidence of imminent harm, and operational capacity. PROOVRA may decline to act on reports that do not contain enough information to assess the matter.

### 4.2 Possible Actions

Where appropriate, PROOVRA may take actions such as:

- requesting additional information from the reporter
- contacting the user or workspace owner whose account is the subject of the report
- restricting access to the relevant content, record, workspace, or account
- removing or disabling content
- suspending or terminating accounts
- preserving records for investigation
- referring the matter to competent authorities where legally required or appropriate
- coordinating with subprocessors involved in the matter

### 4.3 Standard of Review

PROOVRA reviews reports against the Acceptable Use Policy, the Terms of Service, applicable law, and the information available to PROOVRA at the time of review. PROOVRA is not a court and does not adjudicate disputes between users.

### 4.4 Service-Level Expectations

PROOVRA aims to respond to abuse reports in a reasonable time and to prioritise reports that suggest imminent harm. PROOVRA does not guarantee specific resolution times. Use of phrases such as "we aim to" reflects this intention; they should not be read as a binding service-level commitment.

## 5. ESCALATION

Reports involving credible suggestion of imminent risk of death or serious physical harm are escalated for expedited review where reasonably possible. PROOVRA may coordinate with competent authorities in those situations as permitted by law.

## 6. REPEAT-ABUSE HANDLING

Where the same account, workspace, or actor is subject to repeated, substantiated reports, PROOVRA may apply progressive enforcement actions, including expanded restrictions, suspension, or termination, consistent with the Acceptable Use Policy and the Terms of Service.

## 7. ABUSIVE OR FALSE REPORTS

You must not knowingly submit false, misleading, or abusive reports. PROOVRA may take action against actors who use the abuse-reporting channel to harass other users, to suppress lawful content, or to attempt to interfere with the verification system.

## 8. EMERGENCY / SENSITIVE SITUATIONS

For genuine emergencies — risk of death, serious physical harm, or similar imminent harm — please contact local emergency services. PROOVRA is not an emergency-response provider and cannot replace law-enforcement or emergency response.

Where there is a credible suggestion of imminent harm, you may also include the words "URGENT — emergency risk" in the subject line so the report is queued for expedited review.

## 9. LAW-ENFORCEMENT BOUNDARIES

PROOVRA reviews abuse reports operationally. PROOVRA does not:

- conduct law-enforcement investigations
- issue forensic conclusions
- determine factual truth, authorship, identity, intent, or admissibility
- act as a court, regulator, or law-enforcement authority

Where appropriate, PROOVRA may refer matters to competent authorities. Where law-enforcement contact is required, please use the channel described in the Law Enforcement Request Policy.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

PROOVRA seeks to handle abuse reports with reasonable confidentiality. Identifying information about reporters may be shared with affected users or third parties only where required by law, where the reporter has consented, or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to take action on the report.

## 11. NO PRE-SCREENING

PROOVRA does not routinely pre-screen all user content. PROOVRA reviews reports based on available information, legal obligations, and operational capacity.

## 12. CONTACT

Abuse reports: **support@proovra.com**

For other channels, see the Trust Center.

# Copyright and DMCA Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

PROOVRA respects intellectual property rights and expects users of the digital evidence operations platform to do the same. This policy explains how copyright complaints may be submitted and how PROOVRA may respond. It is written to be jurisdiction-neutral enough to support notices framed under the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), the European Union framework on copyright in the digital single market, the German Telemedia Act / Digital Services Act framework, and similar regimes.

This policy is read together with the Acceptable Use Policy, the Abuse Reporting page, the Privacy Policy, and the Terms of Service.

## 1. EVIDENCE PLATFORM CONTEXT

PROOVRA is a digital evidence operations platform. The platform's verification surfaces (Verification Reports, Verification Packages, Public Verification URLs) are designed to allow reviewers to inspect recorded integrity signals. PROOVRA does not routinely review all user-uploaded material in advance and does not pre-screen content.

A copyright complaint should focus on identifiable material hosted through PROOVRA. PROOVRA is not in a position to adjudicate underlying copyright disputes; PROOVRA can act on properly submitted notices and apply the framework set out below.

## 2. COPYRIGHT NOTICE

If you believe content hosted through PROOVRA infringes your copyright, please submit a notice to **legal@proovra.com**.

A complete notice should include:

- your full legal name and contact details (address, telephone, email)
- a clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or, if multiple works at a single online site are covered by a single notice, a representative list of those works)
- a clear identification of the allegedly infringing content, including the URL, record identifier, Public Verification URL, Verification Package identifier, workspace identifier, or other information reasonably sufficient to locate the material
- a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
- a statement, under penalty of perjury where required by applicable law, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorised to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed
- your physical or electronic signature

PROOVRA may, at its discretion, contact the submitter for additional information where a notice is incomplete.

## 3. ACTION ON NOTICE

Where a complete notice is received and the matter falls within the scope of this policy, PROOVRA may, taking into account applicable law and the nature of the platform:

- restrict access to the affected content
- remove or disable the affected content
- preserve records relating to the matter for investigation
- contact the user or workspace owner whose account is the subject of the notice
- coordinate with subprocessors involved in the matter
- refer the matter to competent authorities where appropriate

Action on a notice does not constitute a determination by PROOVRA of any underlying legal question. PROOVRA reserves the right to evaluate notices on the information provided and applicable law.

## 4. COUNTER-NOTICE

If you believe that material was removed, restricted, or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice to **legal@proovra.com** including:

- your full legal name and contact details
- a clear identification of the affected content and the location at which it appeared before removal or restriction
- a statement, under penalty of perjury where required by applicable law, that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification
- your consent to the jurisdiction of an appropriate court, where required by the applicable copyright regime
- a statement that you will accept service of process from the complainant or its agent, where required
- your physical or electronic signature

Subject to applicable law, PROOVRA may restore access to the material after a reasonable period unless the complainant initiates further legal action against the alleged infringer.

## 5. REPEAT INFRINGER POLICY

In appropriate circumstances, and consistent with applicable law, PROOVRA may suspend or terminate the accounts of users that PROOVRA reasonably determines to be repeat infringers. Determinations under this section are made on a case-by-case basis taking into account the totality of available information.

## 6. FRAUDULENT OR ABUSIVE NOTICES

You must not knowingly submit a false, misleading, or abusive copyright notice or counter-notice. Submitters who use this process to harass other users, to suppress lawful content, or to interfere with the platform may be subject to action under the Terms of Service, the Acceptable Use Policy, and applicable law.

## 7. NO PROACTIVE MONITORING

PROOVRA does not routinely review all user-uploaded material in advance. PROOVRA reviews notices when they are submitted and applies the framework in this policy.

## 8. EVIDENCE-INTEGRITY BOUNDARY

PROOVRA's integrity records (hashes, signatures, custody events, timestamp tokens, Verification Reports, Verification Package manifests) are platform records of recorded signals. Removal or restriction of underlying user-facing content under this policy does not, by itself, alter the integrity records associated with that content, except as described in the Data Retention Policy.

## 9. JURISDICTION-NEUTRAL FRAMING

This policy is intended to support notices framed under multiple legal regimes. The specific procedural rights of complainants, alleged infringers, and PROOVRA may differ by jurisdiction, and the operation of this policy is subject to mandatory law. Submitters should consult their own counsel regarding their rights and obligations.

## 10. CONTACT

Copyright notices and counter-notices: **legal@proovra.com**

For non-copyright abuse, see the Abuse Reporting page. For privacy and data-subject requests, see the Privacy Requests page.

# Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-23

PROOVRA is a digital evidence operations platform designed for lawful capture, preservation, hashing, signing, timestamping, custody recording, verification, reporting, and reviewer workflows for digital evidence. This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") describes what is allowed and prohibited when using PROOVRA.

By using PROOVRA, you agree to follow this AUP in addition to the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms), the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), the [AI Use Policy](/legal/ai-use-policy), and any other applicable PROOVRA policies.

## 1. PERMITTED USE

PROOVRA may be used only for lawful purposes. Permitted workflows include:

- documentation and operational recordkeeping
- audit and compliance reviews
- compliance and regulatory documentation
- investigative recordkeeping
- dispute resolution and pre-litigation review
- insurance and claims documentation
- legal review and pre-court preparation
- journalism and source-material preservation
- enterprise governance, internal controls, and policy enforcement
- inter-organisation reviewer workflows where lawfully shared

PROOVRA may be used as one operational input in a larger decision process. PROOVRA is not the legal authority for those processes.

## 2. USER RESPONSIBILITY FOR LAWFUL CAPTURE AND UPLOAD

Users must have the rights, permissions, consent, lawful basis, or authority required before uploading, capturing, storing, sharing, or processing materials involving:

- other people
- devices owned or controlled by others
- property
- workplaces, including remote-work and hybrid contexts
- private communications
- confidential or proprietary information
- health, biometric, children's, employment, financial, or other regulated data

Users are responsible for compliance with applicable recording, workplace, surveillance, privacy, employment, discovery, court, regulatory, intellectual-property, and sector-specific obligations. PROOVRA does not adjudicate whether a user had lawful basis to capture or upload material.

## 3. PROHIBITED CONTENT AND CONDUCT

The following content and conduct is prohibited:

- illegal content
- child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and other exploitative content involving minors
- terrorism, violent extremism, and incitement to mass violence
- malware, exploit code, and malicious payloads
- harassment, stalking, threats, or intimidation
- unlawful surveillance, covert recording in violation of law, and unlawful interception
- privacy and personality-rights violations
- intellectual-property infringement
- fraud, impersonation, and deceptive practices
- doxxing or unlawful disclosure of personal data
- extortion and blackmail
- non-consensual intimate content
- content intended to facilitate harm against persons or critical infrastructure
- regulated data uploaded without a lawful basis (for example, health, biometric, financial, or children's data)
- content that incites discrimination on protected grounds

## 4. EVIDENCE-SPECIFIC MISUSE

Users may not:

- create or package knowingly misleading evidence records
- manipulate evidence records, Verification Reports, Verification Packages, Public Verification URLs, or metadata to deceive recipients
- remove context to misrepresent a record
- present PROOVRA outputs as court certification, legal admissibility guarantee, government approval, forensic conclusion, or proof of factual truth
- falsely claim that PROOVRA verified identity, authorship, intent, lawful provenance, or admissibility
- use verification pages or packages to impersonate courts, lawyers, experts, insurers, regulators, law enforcement, or PROOVRA staff
- present AI-assisted summaries as PROOVRA legal conclusions

See the [Verification Disclaimer](/legal/verification-disclaimer) and [Verification Methodology](/legal/verification-methodology) for the scope of what PROOVRA records and what it does not establish.

## 5. VERIFICATION SURFACE MISUSE

Users may not misuse:

- Public Verification URLs
- Verification Reports
- Verification Packages
- reviewer portals
- access tokens and shared links
- exported reports

Examples of misuse include:

- forwarding restricted links to unauthorized people
- altering screenshots of verification pages
- presenting stale or revoked reports as current
- using reports to mislead customers, employers, insurers, courts, regulators, or public audiences
- packaging verification artifacts in a way designed to imply a legal outcome PROOVRA has not stated

## 6. SECURITY AND PLATFORM ABUSE

The following is prohibited:

- unauthorized access to the platform or to other customers' workspaces
- credential stuffing, brute-force attempts, and account takeover attempts
- scraping or large-scale automated retrieval not authorized by the customer's plan or by contract
- rate-limit bypass or anti-abuse circumvention
- spam
- phishing campaigns operated through PROOVRA surfaces
- distribution of malware
- vulnerability exploitation outside the [Security and Responsible Disclosure](/legal/security) policy
- disruption of service availability
- probing or fuzzing beyond what the Responsible Disclosure policy permits
- attempts to access other customers' data, audit logs, or signing material

Good-faith security research that follows the Security and Responsible Disclosure policy is governed by that policy and its safe-harbor language.

## 7. AI MISUSE

Where AI assistance is enabled, users may not:

- present AI output as legal advice, forensic conclusion, proof of factual truth, authorship, identity, or admissibility
- use AI assistance to fabricate or misrepresent evidence context
- use AI output to bypass required human review
- attempt to extract training data, model weights, or system prompts
- use AI features in violation of provider-level terms or this AUP

See the [AI Use Policy](/legal/ai-use-policy) for the full scope of permitted AI use, including the metadata-first boundary and the no-training commitment for customer evidence content.

## 8. ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS

Where misuse, abuse, fraud, security risk, legal obligation, or material AUP violation is suspected or established, PROOVRA may take any of the following actions, depending on circumstances:

- warn the user or workspace owner
- request clarification or additional information
- restrict sharing of specific records, links, or packages
- disable Public Verification URLs
- remove or disable specific content
- suspend specific features
- suspend the account or workspace
- terminate access
- preserve records for investigation
- notify affected customers
- report the matter to competent authorities where required or appropriate

PROOVRA uses "may" deliberately. Enforcement actions are not automatic; they depend on the nature, severity, and context of the violation.

## 9. PRESERVATION DURING INVESTIGATION

Where misuse, abuse, fraud, security risk, or a legal obligation is suspected, PROOVRA may preserve logs, custody events, Verification Reports, Verification Packages, account records, signing context, and related operational metadata for:

- investigation
- legal compliance
- security
- fraud prevention
- dispute handling
- evidence-integrity preservation

Preservation does not, by itself, authorize disclosure. Disclosure follows the relevant policy (for example, the [Law Enforcement Request Policy](/legal/law-enforcement) for authority requests).

## 10. REPORTING MISUSE

Reports may be routed through the relevant policy:

- [Abuse Reporting](/legal/abuse-reporting) — misuse, unlawful conduct, fraudulent verification use
- [Copyright and DMCA Policy](/legal/dmca) — copyright complaints and counter-notices
- [Law Enforcement Request Policy](/legal/law-enforcement) — legal demands and preservation requests
- [Security and Responsible Disclosure](/legal/security) — vulnerabilities and suspected compromise
- [Privacy Requests](/legal/privacy-requests) — data-subject and privacy requests

When in doubt, reports may be sent to **support@proovra.com** for routing.

## 11. NO GENERAL MONITORING

PROOVRA does not promise proactive review of all user content. PROOVRA may act on:

- reports submitted through the channels above
- automated security and abuse signals
- legal obligations
- customer requests
- operational risk indicators

Absence of action by PROOVRA does not imply approval of content. Users remain responsible for their content and use of the platform.

How a job can reach a BullMQ queue in this repository:
  (a) packages/shared/src/queue-integrity/enqueue.ts  enqueueCanonicalJob  -> the ONLY queue.add() for registry work
  (b) services/api/src/queue/canonical-queue-client.ts enqueueCanonicalWork -> calls (a), keyed by JOB_NAMES.*
  (c) services/worker/src/queue.ts enqueueWork()/enqueue*Job helpers     -> call (a), keyed by JOB_NAMES.*
  (d) a raw 'new Queue(<name>)' + '.add(' anywhere else
So a producer for a queue must reference its QUEUE_NAMES key, its JOB_NAMES key, its
literal name, its worker Queue object, or its enqueue helper. Each is searched below.
